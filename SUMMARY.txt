KEY INNOVATIONS
---------------
1. Modality-Aware Encoding
   - Learnable embeddings for 5 imaging modalities
   - Better cross-modal knowledge transfer

2. Cross-Scale Uncertainty Propagation
   - Multi-layer uncertainty extraction
   - Memory bank for uncertainty aggregation

3. Frequency-Spatial Decomposition Decoder
   - Separate low-freq (structure) and high-freq (details) processing
   - Dual-prompt cross-modal attention

4. Boundary-Aware Contrastive Learning
   - Specialized loss for boundary regions
   - Improved boundary delineation

DATASETS (16 total, 5 modalities)
---------------------------------
Ultrasound:     BUSI, BUSBRA, BUSUC, BUID, UDIAT, EUS (6)
MRI:            BRISC, BTMRI (2)
CT:             Covid19 (1)
Endoscopy:      CVC300, ClinicDB, ColonDB, Kvasir, BKAI (5)
Dermoscopy:     ISIC, UWaterlooSkinCancer (2)
